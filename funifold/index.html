Party games for the people around you. Phones hold the secrets; the fun happens at the table.

The games

Three games, one table

Everyone plays on their own phone, in the same room. No account, nothing to set up.

🎉

Fib or Fact

Everyone writes a believable fib. Spot the real answer, and score when your fib fools your friends.

🔒

Secret Code

Hide a code of gems, then race to crack everyone else's with gold and silver clues.

⭐

Lantern Isles

Order your boats all at once, make pacts, and hold the lantern islands. No dice, just plans.

📱

Join anywhere

Type a 4-digit code, scan a QR code or tap a link. Works on Wi-Fi, a hotspot, or phone to phone when there's no shared Wi-Fi.

⏱️

Play at your pace

Pick 30 seconds to 2½ minutes per round. When time runs out, whatever you've typed or planned still counts.

🤖

Bots when you're alone

Every game can be played with bots on one phone, to learn it or just for fun.

🦊

Your face at the table

Choose from 41 icons or use your own photo. Other players see it only while the room is open.

🔐

Stays at the table

Phones talk directly to each other, never through our servers. No account, and your record stays on your phone.

✋

You let them in

The host sees who wants to join and taps Let in. Nobody gets in without the room code and your OK.

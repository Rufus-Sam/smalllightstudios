Funifold privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Funifold is made by Small Light Studios. It is a set of party games for people in the same room. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • We (Small Light Studios) don't receive or store anything about you. The app has no servers of ours, no analytics and no ads in this version.
  • Your profile and game history stay on your phone.
  • When you play in a room, only game information goes to the other phones in that room, over your own Wi-Fi or hotspot.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile: the name and face (an emoji picture) you choose.
  • Your record: games played, wins and scores.

These are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Playing in a room

When you host or join a room, the phones talk to each other directly over the local Wi-Fi network or a phone's hotspot, not over the internet. - Shared with players in the room: your name and face, your moves and answers, and the scores. - Room code: while a lobby is open, the host phone announces the room code and a port number on the local network, so nearby phones can find it. - Not encrypted: this local game traffic isn't encrypted, so don't type anything private into answers. - Nothing kept: room information isn't kept after the game, except your own record on your own phone.

Other players see what you share during the game and may remember it or take screenshots, just like at a real table.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan a room's QR code to join it. The picture is read on your phone and is never saved or sent anywhere. You can always join by code or link instead.

Sharing an invite

If you share a room link, the app opens your phone's share sheet. What you send, and to whom, is up to you and the app you pick.

Children

Funifold is meant for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds something new, such as ads or purchases, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before that version is released.

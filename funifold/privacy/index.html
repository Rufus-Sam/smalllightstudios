Funifold privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Funifold is made by Small Light Studios. It is a set of party games for people in the same room. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • When the host opens Fib or Fact's game types, the app fetches the list of question packs, and downloads a pack the first time it's picked, from our content site on GitHub Pages (rufus-sam.github.io/funifold-content). Like any website, GitHub sees the phone's internet address for that request; the app sends nothing about you, and no account or ID is involved.
  • At most once a day, the app asks Google Play whether a newer version of Funifold exists (Google's in-app updates, through the Play Store app on your phone). If there is one, Google Play offers to download it. The app sends nothing about you; Google Play's own privacy policy applies.
  • We (Small Light Studios) don't receive or store anything about you. The app has no servers of ours, no analytics and no ads in this version.
  • Your profile and game history stay on your phone.
  • When you play in a room, only game information goes to the other phones in that room, over your Wi-Fi or hotspot, or directly between nearby phones (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct). Never over the internet.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile: the name and picture you choose: an icon, or a photo you pick from your phone. A photo is cropped to a small square and kept in the app's storage.
  • Your record: games played, wins and scores.

These are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Playing in a room

When you host or join a room, the phones talk to each other directly, not over the internet: - Wi-Fi or hotspot: over the local network the phones are on. - Nearby: when the Wi-Fi doesn't let phones see each other (common on office and guest Wi-Fi), or there's no Wi-Fi, phones connect directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct using Google Play services' Nearby Connections. This needs Android's "Nearby devices" permission, and the location permission, which Google's Nearby requires to search for nearby phones. Funifold never reads, stores or sends your location. - Shared with players in the room: your name and picture (including your photo, if you chose one), your moves and answers, and the scores. Other players' phones show your photo only while the room is open and don't save it. - Room code: while a room is open, the host phone announces the room code on the local network and over Nearby, so nearby phones can find it. Joining still needs the host's "Let in". - Encryption: game traffic over Wi-Fi isn’t encrypted (Nearby links are encrypted by Google Play services), so don’t type anything private into answers. - Nothing kept: room information isn't kept after the game, except your own record on your own phone.

Other players see what you share during the game and may remember it or take screenshots, just like at a real table.

Photos

If you choose a photo for your profile picture, you pick it with Android's photo picker, so Funifold only sees that one picture, not your gallery. Choosing an icon instead deletes the photo from the app.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan a room's QR code to join it. The picture is read on your phone and is never saved or sent anywhere. You can always join by code or link instead.

Sharing an invite

If you share a room link, the app opens your phone's share sheet. What you send, and to whom, is up to you and the app you pick. The link opens a small page on smalllightstudios.com that passes the room to Funifold (or offers Google Play). The room details sit after the "#" in the link, so browsers never send them to our website, and the page collects nothing.

Children

Funifold is meant for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds something new, such as ads or purchases, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before that version is released.

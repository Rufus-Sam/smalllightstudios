Finding Life Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Finding Life ("the app") is made by Small Light Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app:

  • has no account or sign-up
  • goes online only to download a language you choose. English is inside the app. When you pick another language, the app downloads that language's Bible and content from GitHub Pages (rufus-sam.github.io), then works offline. Nothing about you is sent; like any website, GitHub sees the device's IP address when it serves the file (see GitHub's privacy statement).
  • at most once a day, asks Google Play whether a newer version of the app exists (Google's in-app updates, through the Play Store app on your phone). If there is one, Google Play offers to download it. The app sends nothing about you; Google Play's own privacy policy applies.
  • contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools
  • does not sell or share any data, because it never has any

What stays on your device

Everything you add or choose is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • the name you give on the welcome screen (optional)
  • the path and struggles you choose on the welcome screen
  • your journey progress, daily goals and streak
  • verses and stories you save
  • your settings (language, appearance, text size), your chosen journey and your home region
  • the languages you download
  • your quiz history: each quiz's settings, when you took it, your answers, your score and your retakes
  • the Leaders lessons you mark as done

We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it. It may be included in your device's own backups (Google or iCloud), which Google and Apple handle under their own privacy policies.

Feedback by email

Send feedback in Settings, and the flag on a story, a journey day, a help topic or chosen verses, open your own email app with a message to hello@smalllightstudios.com. It includes the app version, the app's language, your phone's operating system version and, for a report, which item it's about. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself; we use what you write only to reply and to fix the app.

Quiz challenges

Challenge a friend shares a link through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). The link holds the quiz's settings, your score and, if you've set one, your name, so your friend's app can build the same questions. Nothing is sent unless you share it yourself. The code sits after the # in the link, so the web page that opens it (on smalllightstudios.com) never receives it; that page collects nothing.

Camera and photos

Accept challenge → Scan QR uses the camera, only while you scan, to read a friend's quiz QR code; you can pick a picture of the code instead. The image is read on your phone (with the open-source zxing library) and is never saved or sent anywhere. The app asks for the camera only when you tap Scan QR, and works without it.

Installing from a challenge link

If a challenge link sends you to Google Play to install the app, Google Play passes the challenge code to the app (the "install referrer"), so its first launch can open that challenge. The app reads it on your phone and sends nothing.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because it discusses subjects such as suicide, addiction and abuse, it is meant for ages 13 and up.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
